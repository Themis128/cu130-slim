## Content Planning Guidelines
1. **Mix formats:** Combine short-form tips (Twitter/X, TikTok), medium-depth posts (LinkedIn, blog), and long-form guides (YouTube tutorials, case studies)
2. **Batch creation:** Produce multiple pieces of content per pillar during dedicated creation sessions
3. **User-generated content:** Encourage community submissions for Pillar 5 and feature them in Pillar 3
4. **Cross-pillar references:** Link related content (e.g., a Product Showcase video can reference Tips & Tricks used in the demo)
5. **Metrics tracking:** Monitor engagement per pillar to refine focus over time
